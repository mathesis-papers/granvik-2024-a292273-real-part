Proof of Granvik's real-part conjecture in OEIS A292273 (revision 24, FORMULA, 6 July 2024)

Haobo Ma, Wenlin Zhang

Published paper · Version 2 · CC BY 4.0

PDFSourceCitationRepositoryMathesis recordLean source

Abstract

We prove Mats Granvik's conjecture in the FORMULA section of OEIS A292273, revision 24, dated 6 July 2024: the sequence that is zero at odd positive indices and -mu(n) at even indices equals Re(mu(n)i^n). The proof uses the four powers of i and the vanishing of the Möbius function on multiples of four. We also derive the adjacent parity and support formulas, prove a finite-trajectory identity for every map whose even branch halves and whose odd branch is even, and give the associated Dirichlet identities and support density. We correct the odd-prime-power formula and two reciprocal constants in the related entry A087003.
